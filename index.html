Piper Alpha: When a Permit-to-Work System Failed 167 People
Offshore PlatformProcess Safety · 1988
What Happened
167 men died because one conversation didn't happen at shift handover. The night crew started a pump without knowing the safety valve had been removed. Nobody told them.
Root Cause
Suspended permit not communicated at shift handover.
Lesson Learned
A PTW system is a communication protocol — not a document.
Action for todayWalk your PTW board. Tell the incoming shift verbally — not on paper.
