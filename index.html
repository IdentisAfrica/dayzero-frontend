Today's Edition
Port Talbot, Wales · 1999
Confined SpaceReviewed by our HSE team
What Happened
Three workers died after entering a confined space to rescue a colleague overcome by nitrogen. None had breathing apparatus and the space had not been tested.
Immediate Cause
Entry into an oxygen-deficient atmosphere without testing or apparatus.
Contributing Factors
No gas test. No rescue plan. Instinctive rescue without protection.
Root Cause
Confined-space entry procedure not enforced as a hard control.
Lesson Learned
Most confined-space deaths are would-be rescuers.
